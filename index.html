A three-tier scheduler that classifies every process by clinical urgency — Emergency, Interactive, Background — and combines Preemptive Priority, Round Robin, and FCFS with a promotion-based aging mechanism so administrative work never starves.

Emergency queue

ECG alerts, ICU monitors, code-blue signals. Preemptive priority scheduling guarantees zero queuing delay for genuine clinical alerts.

Policy: Preemptive Priority
🖥

Interactive queue

Doctor dashboards, EMR lookups, nurse charting. Round Robin with a short quantum keeps the clinician-facing UI responsive and fair.

Policy: Round Robin (4-tick quantum)
🗄

Background queue

Billing batches, nightly backups, reports. FCFS by default, with aging that promotes a long-waiting job into Interactive so it never starves.

Policy: FCFS + promotion-based aging
